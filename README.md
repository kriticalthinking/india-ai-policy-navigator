# India AI Policy Navigator

**An open-source, structured repository of AI policy documents released by Indian central and state governments.**

Built for policy researchers, government officials, and the public interest technology ecosystem.

---

## What This Is

A curated, metadata-rich index of every significant AI-related policy, legislation, advisory, and guideline released by Indian government bodies — central ministries, regulators, and state governments.

For each document you get: a plain-English summary, who it applies to, key obligations, the official source link, and an archived PDF.

## Who It's For

- **Policy researchers** studying India's AI governance landscape
- **Government officials** checking applicable frameworks before deploying AI systems
- **Public sector tech companies** needing a compliance reference
- **Civil society and journalists** tracking how India is regulating AI

## Structure

| Folder | Contents |
|--------|----------|
| `/data` | `policies.csv` — master metadata index of all documents |
| `/docs` | Archived PDFs of all policy documents |
| `/summaries` | Plain-English sector summaries and explainers |

## Coverage

Currently tracking policies from: NITI Aayog, MeitY, IndiaAI Mission, SEBI, TRAI, ICMR, RBI, MoHFW, Ministry of Agriculture, MoHUA, and state governments including Maharashtra, Telangana, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu.

## How to Contribute

Found a policy document that's missing? Please [open an Issue](../../issues/new) with:
- Name of the document
- Issuing body
- Release date
- Official URL

Pull requests with new entries in `policies.csv` format are very welcome.
