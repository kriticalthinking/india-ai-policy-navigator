Digital Personal Data Protection Rules

Subordinate rules under the DPDP Act 2023 that will specify operational details: consent managers, significant data fiduciary criteria, data localisation requirements, and grievance redressal timelines.

Issuing body: MeitY  |  Status: Announced — draft rules circulated for internal review  |  Expected: 2025–26

Strategy for AI in Healthcare for India (SAHI)

MoHFW's forthcoming health-sector-specific AI strategy covering clinical validation standards, AI procurement in public hospitals, and accountability frameworks for diagnostic AI.

Issuing body: MoHFW / NHA  |  Status: Under development  |  Expected: 2025–26

IRDAI AI Guidelines for Insurance Sector

Pending guidelines from the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority on AI use in underwriting, claims processing, fraud detection, and customer-facing insurance chatbots.

Issuing body: IRDAI  |  Status: Working group formed — guidelines pending finalisation  |  Expected: 2025

India AI Safety Institute — Framework & Mandate

Formal establishment of India's AI Safety Institute under the IndiaAI Mission's Safe and Trusted AI pillar. Will develop evaluation methodologies, red-teaming frameworks, and safety benchmarks.

Issuing body: IndiaAI / MeitY  |  Status: Call for partners issued — institute under formation  |  Expected: 2025–26

Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) AI Risk Taxonomy

BIS is working on AI risk taxonomy and certification standards aligned with international frameworks (ISO 42001, EU AI Act). Will underpin procurement requirements for government AI systems.

Issuing body: BIS / DPIIT  |  Status: Standards development in progress  |  Expected: 2025–26

RBI Guidelines for AI in Banking Operations

Following the FREE-AI framework released in August 2025, RBI is expected to release binding guidelines for banks on AI governance, model risk management, and consumer disclosure requirements.

Issuing body: RBI  |  Status: Expected as follow-on to FREE-AI framework  |  Expected: 2026

About This Project

India AI Policy Navigator is an open-source, community-maintained repository of every significant AI-related policy, legislation, advisory, and guideline released by Indian government bodies — central ministries, regulators, and state governments.

There is no single place where policy researchers, government officials, or the technology ecosystem can find all of India's AI governance documents with structured metadata, plain-English summaries, and reliable links. This project exists to fix that.

Who It's For

Policy researchers studying India's AI governance landscape and tracking its evolution over time. Government officials checking which frameworks apply before deploying AI systems in their departments. Public sector technology companies doing compliance due diligence. Civil society and journalists tracking how India is regulating AI.

What We Track

Central government strategy documents, legislation, rules and notifications, regulatory circulars and advisories, sector-specific guidelines, state government AI policies, and announced-but-unreleased policies. We cover NITI Aayog, MeitY, IndiaAI Mission, SEBI, TRAI, ICMR, RBI, MoHFW, Ministry of Agriculture, MoHUA, CDSCO, AICTE, and state governments including Maharashtra, Telangana, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu.

How It Works

All data lives in a public GitHub repository as a structured CSV file. The website reads from that file directly. Anyone can fork the repo, use the data in their own tools, or submit pull requests to add new documents.

Limitations

This repository is maintained on a best-effort basis and is not affiliated with any government body. It may not capture every document. Always verify obligations against official government sources before making compliance decisions.
