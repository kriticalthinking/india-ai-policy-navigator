About This Project

India's AI governance landscape is evolving rapidly — across central ministries, sectoral regulators, and state governments. But there is no single, structured, and publicly accessible repository where these documents can be found, compared, and tracked over time.

The India AI Policy Navigator is an open-source effort to fill that gap. It provides a searchable index of AI-related policy documents issued by Indian government bodies, with structured metadata, plain-language summaries, and direct links to official sources.

Who This Is For

This resource is designed for policy researchers studying the trajectory of India's AI governance, technologists and product teams who need a quick reference on which frameworks apply to their work, organisations conducting regulatory due diligence, and civil society actors and journalists tracking how India is approaching AI regulation.

What We Cover

The index tracks central government strategy documents and legislation, regulatory circulars and advisories, sector-specific guidelines spanning finance, health, telecom, agriculture, education, defence, and urban governance, state-level AI policies, and announced but not-yet-released frameworks. Issuing bodies currently covered include MeitY, NITI Aayog, IndiaAI Mission, SEBI, RBI, TRAI, ICMR, MoHFW, and several state governments.

Methodology and Limitations

This project is maintained on a best-effort, volunteer basis and is not affiliated with any government body. While we strive for accuracy, the index may be incomplete or contain errors. Document summaries and obligation descriptions are editorial interpretations and should not be treated as legal advice. Users should always consult official government sources before making compliance decisions.

How It Works

All data is maintained as a structured CSV file in a public GitHub repository. The website reads directly from this file, ensuring full transparency. The dataset is freely available for researchers, developers, and organisations to fork, extend, or integrate into their own tools.

How to Cite

India AI Policy Navigator (year). Open-source index of AI-related policies of the Government of India and Indian states. https://kriticalthinking.github.io/india-ai-policy-navigator/ (accessed [date]). Individual policy documents should be cited from their official sources.
